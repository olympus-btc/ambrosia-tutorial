# Sale

## 1. Start a Sale

- Dashboard → **Sale**
- Left: your products
- Right: the order **Summary** and the payment

If no shift is open, Ambrosia asks you to open one first (**Open Shift Required**). Enter the **Starting amount in cash register**, the cash you have in the drawer or `0`, and click **Open Shift**. The shift stays open for the next sales. To close it and count the cash, see [Shifts](../tutorials/shifts.md).

On a phone, tap **Checkout** to open the summary.

## 2. Add Products

Click **Add** on any product card. If the product has variants, the **Select variant** window opens: pick the option and click **Add to cart**.

## 3. Adjust or Remove Items

- **Quantity**: type the new quantity or use the arrows.
- **Remove**: the trash icon removes the item, and **Remove All Items** empties the order.

Totals update automatically.

## 4. Add a Discount (Optional)

You only see this option if your role has the **Apply discounts** permission.

1. Next to **Discount**, click **Add**.
2. Choose **%** for a percentage or switch to a fixed amount, and enter the value.
3. Click **Apply**. Use **Remove** to take the discount off.

## 5. Add a Tip (Optional)

If tips are enabled, the **Tip** section shows the suggested percentages, **No tip** and **Custom**. With **Custom** you enter a percentage or a fixed amount and click **Apply**.

To turn tips on or off or change the suggested percentages, go to **Settings** → **Business** → **Tips**.

## 6. Review Order

Check the items, quantities, **Subtotal**, discount, tip and **Total**.

## 7. Select Payment Method

**BTC (Lightning)** is selected by default. The list also includes **Cash**, **Credit Card**, **Debit Card** and **Bank Transfer**. Pick one and click **Pay**.

## 8. Complete Payment

### Bitcoin (Lightning)

1. The **Pay with Bitcoin (Lightning)** window shows a QR invoice with the total in your currency and in sats.
2. The customer scans the QR and pays.
3. When the payment arrives, the window shows **Payment confirmed**. Click **Close**.

If the live connection drops while you wait, a **Mark as paid** button appears so you can register a payment you know arrived.

### Cash

1. In **Cash Payment**, enter the **Cash received**. Ambrosia shows the **Change**.
2. Click **Confirm payment**.

### Credit or Debit Card

1. **Card Payment** shows the **Total due** and the card method you chose.
2. Charge the card on your card terminal and click **Confirm payment**.

### Bank Transfer

1. In **Bank Transfer Payment**, enter the transfer's **Reference number** (optional).
2. Click **Confirm payment**.

A success message confirms the sale.
