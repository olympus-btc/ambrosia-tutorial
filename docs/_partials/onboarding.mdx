## Step 1: Choose Your Business Type

The first screen asks **What type of business do you have?** **Store** is already selected, so click **Next**. Restaurant and Freelancer are marked as coming soon.

:::info
Moving from another Ambrosia installation? Click **Restoring from a previous backup?** on this screen to restore a backup file exported from it instead of starting from scratch (see [Backup and Restore](../tutorials/backup-and-restore.md)).
:::

## Step 2: Create Your Admin Account

This account has full access to all features and settings. **Keep these credentials safe**: you need them to manage your store. Later you can add a user for each person on your team: see [Users and Roles](../tutorials/users-and-roles.md).

**User Name**
```
Admin
```

**Password** and **Confirm Password**

It protects access to the wallet and must be at least 8 characters long and include uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers and special characters.
```
Ambrosia2026!
```

**PIN**

The 4 digits you use to log in to the app.
```
0000
```

## Step 3: Enter Your Store Details

These details appear on receipts, reports and throughout the system.

**Store name**
```
Lightning Electronics
```

**Currency** and **Timezone** are also required, but they come pre-filled: USD and the timezone detected on your device. Change them if they don't match your store.

Optional fields:

- Address
- Phone
- Email
- Tax ID
- Store logo (PNG, JPG or GIF, up to 5 MB)

## Step 4: Choose the Lightning Backend

Keep **phoenixd** selected and leave **Remote phoenixd node** turned off. This tutorial uses the phoenixd node that comes with your installation.

:::info
Ambrosia can also receive payments through **Nostr Wallet Connect** (any NWC-compatible wallet, such as Alby Hub) or a **Remote phoenixd node** running elsewhere. The rest of this tutorial assumes phoenixd.
:::

## Step 5: Add Extra Security (Optional)

Checking **Encrypt phoenixd password, NWC connection, and notification key with a password** protects those secrets with an **Unlock password**. Write it down somewhere safe: it can't be recovered, and you may need to enter it again when the app restarts. While it's locked, the app asks you to unlock it in **Settings** → **Secrets encryption**.

You can leave it unchecked and turn it on later from **Settings**.

## Step 6: Review and Finish

Check the **Summary of your setup**, use **Edit** to fix any section, and click **Finish**. A success message appears, then the page reloads and shows the login screen.

## First Login

1. Under **Employee Selection**, choose your user.
2. Enter your 4-digit PIN.
3. Click **Log In**.
