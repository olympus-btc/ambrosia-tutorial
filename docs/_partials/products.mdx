# Managing Products

## 1. Open the Products Page
- From the Dashboard → **Products**
- You’ll see the list of existing products (name, price, stock, actions).

## 2. Add a Product

### Step 1 — Click "Add Product"
Top-right button.

### Step 2 — Fill the fields

**Product Name** and **Product Price** are required, and so is **Stock** while **Track stock** is on.

**Product Name**
```
5 Sticker Pack
```

**Product Description** (optional)
```
Pack with 5 random stickers
```

**Product Category** (optional): type a name and choose **+ Create "Stickers"** to create the category on the spot. A product can have more than one category.

```
Stickers
```

**SKU** (optional)
```
PK-STICK-05
```

**Product Price**: in your store's currency.
```
1
```

**Stock**
```
20
```

Turn off **Track stock** for products you don't count, such as services.

**Image** (optional): click **Upload an Image** and choose a PNG, JPG or GIF up to 5 MB (square recommended).

:::info
The form also has **Is a Bundle** and **Has variants**, for products made of other products or sold in several versions, such as sizes or colors. Categories can also be managed from the **Categories** section below the product list.
:::

### Step 3 — Click “Add”
Product appears immediately in the list.

## 3. Edit a Product

1. In the product list, click **Edit** on the product.
2. Change what you need and click **Save**.

## 4. Delete a Product

1. Click **Delete** on the product.
2. Confirm with **Delete**. This action cannot be undone.

A product that is a component of a bundle can't be deleted while it's part of that bundle.
