1. Go to **Wallet**, enter your wallet password in **Confirm Wallet Access** and click **Access**.
2. In the **Receive** tab, make sure **Sats** is selected and enter the amount:

   ```
   5000
   ```

3. Add a description (optional), for example:

   ```
   Channel open
   ```

4. Click **Create Lightning Invoice**.
5. Scan the invoice with your Lightning wallet and pay it. You should see a confirmation on the screen.

:::warning
Make sure you create the invoice for 5000 sats to open the channel, otherwise the payment could fail.
:::

:::info
These sats cover the mining fee to open a channel with ACINQ. They are not refundable.
:::

You can replay the app's guided tour of these steps from **Settings** → **Help**: click **Replay** next to **Lightning Channel**.
