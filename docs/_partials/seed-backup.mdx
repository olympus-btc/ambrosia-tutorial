## Back Up Your Recovery Phrase

:::warning
Do this before depositing any funds. Your recovery phrase (seed) is the only way to recover your funds if your device is lost, reset or damaged. Write it down, store it offline somewhere safe and never share it with anyone.
:::

1. Go to **Settings** → **Bitcoin & Wallet**.
2. In the **Lightning SEED** card, click **View SEED**.
3. Enter your **Wallet password** and click **Confirm**.
4. Write the words on paper, in order. Don't save them in digital format.

You can replay the app's guided tour about the seed from **Settings** → **Help**: click **Replay** next to **SEED Phrase**.
